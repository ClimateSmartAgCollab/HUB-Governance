# Curation Guidebook

## Action

*   An action should be defined in general, and then specialized with expressions about features that are specific to the prohibition, duty or permission rule they are ensconced in.
*    An action is a behaviour/process that an agent (person / organization / bot) is engaged in as a participant. Agent roles (names for types of participant) vary among disciplines, and so vocabulary extensions may be needed to capture these adequately. Ideally a hierarchy of kinds of participant is constructed, one which all ODRL extensions can take advantage of, since each domain may have variations on more general roles, i.e. which require particular qualifications or capabilities, skills or occupations. A rule may may merit or require participation of an agent bearing a kind of role.
*    Inspired by a material processing model,
*    An action may transform a resource either by creating a new entity, adding components to one, taking parts away (even destroying it), or modifying its characteristics.
*    An action may change a resource's context, e.g. relocating it, copying it to a new context, or even just referencing it.
*    An action may have constraints on frequency, time of operation, or other dependent actions (processes) as parts or as preceding actions.
